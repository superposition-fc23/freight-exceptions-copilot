# Shipment exception SOP (sample)

Exceptions: delay over 48 hours, customs hold, damaged cargo, rolled booking, missing documents.
Delays over 48 hours: notify the customer within 4 business hours with a new ETA and cause.
Rolled bookings: offer the next sailing and the cost of an alternative carrier.
Damaged cargo: collect photos within 24 hours and open a claim with the carrier within 3 days.
An operator must approve any customer credit above 500 USD; below that the system may issue it.
