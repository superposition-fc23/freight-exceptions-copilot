# Demurrage and detention policy (sample)

Demurrage is charged by the carrier when a container stays at the terminal past its free time.
Standard free time at US West Coast ports is 4 calendar days from discharge. After free time,
demurrage is 150 USD per container per day for days 1 to 4, and 300 USD per day from day 5.
Detention is charged when the container is outside the terminal past the agreed return date.
Customers are notified when a container is 1 day from the end of its free time.
Disputes must be raised within 30 days of the invoice date with proof of a terminal delay.
