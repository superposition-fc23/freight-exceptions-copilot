# Customs holds (sample)

A customs hold stops a container from leaving the port until CBP releases it.
Common causes: missing or wrong HTS code, value mismatch between commercial invoice and entry,
missing ISF filing (due 24 hours before vessel loading), or a random exam.
An exam hold usually adds 3 to 10 days. Exam fees are paid by the importer of record.
The broker must respond to a CBP information request within 2 business days.
Time spent on a customs exam does not pause demurrage unless the carrier agrees in writing.
