Shipment exceptions dashboard

The consignee planner sees every open exception as it happens and is sent to the portal page where they act on it. Ops is the backstop for anything the dashboard abstains on, and the source of truth for whether a suggestion was right.

Planner view. Four columns, one card per shipment per exception type (R1). The selected card shows up to 3 suggested actions, each with its cited playbook passage and a portal link (R3, N1, N2).
